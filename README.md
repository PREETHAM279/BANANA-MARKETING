# BANANA-MARKETING

## Bengaluru Sales CRM

A private CRM that runs on your Mac, with 100 researched Bengaluru businesses ready for outreach. You use it to prospect for two products: a Social Media Management app and an Employee Attendance app.

- **Open:** double-click `Start Bengaluru CRM.command` (or `bengaluru-crm/start-crm.command`). Your browser opens http://localhost:3100.
- **On your phone:** in the CRM on your Mac, go to Settings & Data → Phone access, turn it on and scan the QR code with your phone (same Wi-Fi, or anywhere with Tailscale). The phone needs a passcode, and nothing is put on the internet.
- **Full guide:** [bengaluru-crm/README.md](bengaluru-crm/README.md). It covers setup, restarting, backups, exports and the optional Google and email integrations.
- **The 100 businesses:** [bengaluru-crm/RESEARCHED_BUSINESSES.md](bengaluru-crm/RESEARCHED_BUSINESSES.md)
